About Routel

Integrated power, connectivity and security for homes, offices, estates and remote sites.

Routel Solutions and Consults Ltd is an engineering and technology company specialising in solar energy, network infrastructure and surveillance systems. We design, install and maintain the systems that keep homes, businesses, estates and remote sites powered, connected and protected.

By delivering electrical, networking and security work through a single team, we remove the burden of coordinating multiple contractors. Clients gain one point of accountability, better coordination between workstreams, and installations that are clean, labelled and tested before handover.

Our commitment continues beyond commissioning. We provide ongoing maintenance and support so that every system performs reliably for the long term.
